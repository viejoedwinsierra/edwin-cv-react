# Certification inventory

The structured certification catalog is in `src/data/core/certifications.js`.

The original evidence files were reviewed from the uploaded certification ZIP and are referenced by `sourceFile` only. They are intentionally not copied into this project to avoid duplicating personal evidence in the public CV repository.

Certification evidence is classified as `training` or `corporate-training`. This does not imply professional hands-on experience unless the same technology is also supported by the professional experience dataset.
